# Cycle 10 Theme: Observation Space Design

## Focus
Research best practices for observation space design in robotics RL, focusing on what information to include and how to represent it.

## Context
- Warehouser has 3 observation versions (V1 position, V2 lidar, V3 multi-robot)
- V1 uses ground truth positions (unrealistic for real deployment)
- Need to understand observation space design for sim-to-real transfer

## Objectives
1. Research observation space design patterns in robotics RL
2. Study sensor fusion approaches
3. Identify temporal/history observation patterns
4. Document normalization and encoding strategies
5. Find patterns for task-relevant observations
