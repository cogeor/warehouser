# Cycle 12 Theme: Reward Shaping Patterns

## Focus
Research reward shaping best practices for robotics RL, focusing on effective reward design and avoiding common pitfalls.

## Context
- Warehouser has a modular RewardCalculator with Strategy pattern
- Multiple reward components: navigation, collision, time, pick/place, exploration
- Need to understand reward engineering best practices

## Objectives
1. Research reward shaping techniques and pitfalls
2. Study sparse vs dense reward trade-offs
3. Identify reward normalization strategies
4. Document multi-objective reward balancing
5. Find patterns for curriculum reward schedules
