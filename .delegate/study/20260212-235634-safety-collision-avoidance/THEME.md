# Cycle 17 Theme: Safety and Collision Avoidance

## Focus
Research safety systems and collision avoidance patterns for warehouse robots.

## Context
- Warehouser has a SafetyController that's implemented but not integrated
- Collision detection exists but no avoidance
- Need robust safety for sim-to-real deployment

## Objectives
1. Research safety architectures for autonomous robots
2. Study collision avoidance algorithms (VFH, DWA, ORCA)
3. Identify safety certification patterns (ISO standards)
4. Document emergency stop and recovery patterns
5. Find patterns for safe RL policy deployment
