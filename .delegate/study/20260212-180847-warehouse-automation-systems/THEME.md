# Cycle 5 Theme: Warehouse Automation Systems

## Focus
Research real-world warehouse automation systems to understand industry patterns, workflows, and integration points.

## Context
- Warehouser simulates warehouse robots
- Need to understand real warehouse operations
- Focus on how simulation translates to production systems

## Objectives
1. Research major warehouse automation companies (Amazon, Kiva, Ocado, AutoStore)
2. Study warehouse workflow patterns (picking, putaway, replenishment)
3. Identify integration points (WMS, WCS, WES)
4. Document industry-standard interfaces and protocols
5. Find architectural patterns for warehouse systems
